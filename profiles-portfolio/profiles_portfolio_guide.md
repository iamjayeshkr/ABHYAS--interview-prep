## 🧮 Part 3: LeetCode Mastery Roadmap

For a 10+ LPA package in India, passing the initial coding Online Assessment (OA) is the primary bottleneck.

### 1. The Strategy
- **Maintain a Streak**: Commit to solving **at least 2 questions daily**. The green contribution grid on LeetCode acts as a badge of honor during interviews.
- **Avoid Tutorial Hell**: Solve questions by writing code. If stuck, look at the editorial or Striver's video *after* 30 minutes, understand the concept, close the solution, and write it yourself from scratch.
- **Milestone Goals**:
  - **Day 15**: 40 Solved (35 Easy, 5 Medium)
  - **Day 30**: 90 Solved (60 Easy, 30 Medium)
  - **Day 45**: 150 Solved (80 Easy, 70 Medium)
